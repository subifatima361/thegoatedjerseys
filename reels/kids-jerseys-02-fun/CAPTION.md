## Look
- Fast cuts (0.5-1s per shot), opening on the Barça Lamine Yamal 10 kit as the hook
- Bright, saturated, punchy grade for a fun kids feel
- Mix of soft dreamy light leaks (warm/pink glows) and bold lens flares (anamorphic streak sweeping across)
- White flash cuts, punch-in zooms and light motion blur on action shots
- Natural park sound (kids playing), loudness-normalised
- Ends on the pastel pink/teal kit close-up in slow-mo with a light leak and fade out
