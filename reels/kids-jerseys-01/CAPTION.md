# Kids Jerseys Reel #1: posting kit

**Files**
- `thegoatedjerseys-kids-reel-cinematic.mp4`: final reel (26.8s, 1080x1920, 30fps, no on-screen text)
- `build.py`: rebuilds it from the raw Drive clips (`python3 build.py <raw_dir> <out.mp4>`)

## Look
Modelled on kids-apparel campaign films (candid play, kids as the heroes):
- Opens on the Barça Lamine Yamal 10 back print as the hook, then a run of Barça shots
- Light, mostly neutral matte grade: lifted blacks, soft highlight roll-off, jersey colours kept true
- Slow motion (0.5x, from 60fps source) on laughs, jumps and fabric; 0.6-0.9x elsewhere for rhythm
- Light motion blur on the jump, the Yamal play shot and the girl in blue 10
- Cropped in on the jerseys on wide shots
- Hard cuts, fade to black at the end, natural park sound at real speed

## Posting tips
- **Cover:** the Lamine Yamal 10 back shot (first frame).
- **Audio:** the reel carries the kids' park sound. A soft, nostalgic track at low volume underneath suits the slow-mo look if you want music.
- **Pin a comment:** "Comment your kid's name + number and we'll mock it up 👕" to drive comments + DMs.
- Get parental consent before posting identifiable kids' faces.
