## Posting tips
- **Audio:** the reel carries the kids' natural park audio at low volume. In Instagram, add a trending upbeat track and drop "Original audio" to ~10–20% — trending audio is the biggest reach lever.
- **Cover:** use the Lamine Yamal 10 back shot (~0:03) or the Zahra 02 back shot (~0:09) as the cover frame.
- **Post time:** evenings / weekend mornings when parents scroll.
- **Pin a comment:** "Comment your kid's name + number and we'll mock it up 👕" to drive comments + DMs.
- Get parental consent before posting identifiable kids' faces.
