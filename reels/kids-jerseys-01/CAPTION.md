**Files**

- `build.py`: rebuilds it from the raw Drive clips (`python3 build.py <raw_dir> <out.mp4>`)

## Look
Modelled on kids-apparel campaign films (candid play, kids as the heroes, warm nostalgic "summer haze"):
- Warm, matte grade: warmer white balance, lifted blacks, soft highlight roll-off, jersey colours kept true
- Slow motion (0.5x, from 60fps source) on laughs, hair, jumps and fabric close-ups; 0.8-0.9x on crest/print shots to keep rhythm
- Hard cuts, short fade in from / out to black
- Natural park sound at real speed under the slow-mo

## Posting tips
- **Cover:** the Lamine Yamal 10 back shot (~0:05) or the girl laughing in France 10 (~0:01).
- **Audio:** the reel carries the kids' park sound. A soft, nostalgic track at low volume underneath suits the slow-mo look if you want music.
- **Pin a comment:** "Comment your kid's name + number and we'll mock it up 👕" to drive comments + DMs.
- Get parental consent before posting identifiable kids' faces.
